Your fingers

Each fingerprint lives in its own numbered slot on the sensor itself — your Mac never sees the prints. Pick a slot, give it a name you'll recognize, and follow the ring.

    If something's off

    • Ring never turns purple? The sensor isn't talking. Check all six wires — especially the white one. It's the one everyone skips, and without it the sensor can hear but can't speak.
    • Ring turned yellow? The device matched your finger but couldn't reach your Mac. Make sure the helper is running.
    • Nothing on this page? The helper serves this page; if it stopped, so did this.
